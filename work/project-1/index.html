Project

Koibito

UI Designer · Writer Windows · Released
Summary

Koibito is an educational visual novel where you play as Yu Miyazaki, an American exchange student in Japan who speaks virtually zero Japanese. You survive dates through linguistic back-channeling, bluff your way into the hearts of others, and — if you're lucky — pick up some real Japanese pitch accent along the way.

I contributed as UI designer and writer on a team of nine, working on a game that hides language learning inside the pressures of a dating sim.
